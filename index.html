Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026   |   Package:com.zeeshandevtech.dailykharch1

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how DailyKharch ("the app", "we") handles information when you use our application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

DailyKharch is a personal expense tracker. It is designed to work entirely offline, and we do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All your data stays on your device. We never see it, collect it, or send it anywhere.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any identifying details.

The only data in the app is what you enter yourself:

  • Expense and income amounts you add
  • Category selections (Food, Transport, Shopping, Other)
  • Timestamps of transactions

This data is stored locally in a database on your device only. It is never uploaded to any server.

Internet and Network Access

DailyKharch does not require an internet connection to function. The app does not make any network requests.

We do not connect to any remote servers, analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party APIs.

Advertising

DailyKharch contains no advertisements of any kind. There are no banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded ads, or any other advertising integrations in this app.

Third-Party Services

DailyKharch does not integrate any third-party SDKs for analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any other purpose. No data is shared with any third party.

Data Storage and Security

All data you enter is stored in a local Room database on your Android device. This data is accessible only through the DailyKharch app.

You can delete all your data at any time from the app's Settings screen using the "Clear All Data" option. Uninstalling the app will also permanently remove all stored data.

Children's Privacy

DailyKharch does not knowingly collect any information from children or from any user. Since the app collects no personal data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the revised policy. Continued use of the app after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
